## SideQuest: Model-Driven KV Cache Management for Long-Horizon Agentic Reasoning

📂 方向：Context Management + AI Agent
👤 作者：Sanjay Kariyappa | G. Edward Suh et al.
📅 日期：2026-02-26
🔗 PDF：https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.22603v1
🔗 ArXiv：https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.22603

💡 **摘要**：Long-running agentic tasks require multi-hop reasoning over multiple documents, causing KV cache to grow rapidly. SideQuest leverages the Large Reasoning Model itself to perform KV cache compression, avoiding heuristic failures in multi-step reasoning contexts.

## Neural Paging: Learning Context Management Policies for Turing-Complete Agents

📂 方向：Context Management
👤 作者：Liang Chen | Qi Liu et al.
📅 日期：2026-02-11
🔗 PDF：https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.02228v1
🔗 ArXiv：https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.02228

💡 **摘要**：Neural Paging introduces a hierarchical architecture that decouples symbolic reasoning from information resource management, treating the context window as a scarce semantic cache. It proposes the Context Paging Problem (CPP) and a lightweight policy for managing context as a finite resource.
