## Structured Distillation for Personalized Agent Memory: 11x Token Reduction with Retrieval Preservation

📂 方向：Agent Memory
👤 作者：Sydney Lewis et al.
📅 日期：2026-03-13
🔗 PDF：https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.13017v1
🔗 ArXiv：https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.13017

💡 **摘要**：Compresses conversation history into compound objects (exchange_core, specific_context, thematic room_assignments, files_touched), averaging 38 tokens per exchange. Applied to 4,182 conversations achieves 11x token reduction while preserving retrieval quality.
